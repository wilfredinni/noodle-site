Noodle ships with **35 built-in themes** and uses the Noodle theme by default
when no theme is saved. Press **Ctrl+T** to open the theme picker and browse
available options.

<Image src={noodleRosepine} alt="Theming" loading="eager" fetchpriority="high" />

Themes cover all UI elements: panes, borders, text, selection, sidebar
badges, status bar, and dialog overlays.

![A selection of Noodle's built-in themes](../../../../assets/themes.png)

## Follow your terminal

Choose **system** in the theme picker to use your terminal's foreground,
background, and ANSI palette. Noodle refreshes its colors when the terminal
reports a palette or theme change during the session.

If the terminal cannot provide its colors, Noodle uses its default colors and
keeps `system` as the saved preference. Palette detection depends on terminal
support.
