Noodle 0.10.1 makes Collection Runner results available as requests finish.
You can inspect a completed response, check its assertions and tests, or read
Console logs while later requests continue. Stop controls and workspace resume
let you follow a run without staying on its screen.

## Inspect results while requests run

Press **F5**, select requests or folders, and configure the environment, tags,
fail-fast behavior, delay, or optional CSV/JSON data file. Start the run and
follow pending, running, passed, failed, skipped, and cancelled rows as they
update. Data-driven runs keep each execution associated with its iteration.

Select a completed row and press **Enter**, or click it, to inspect its response,
script, capture, assertion, and test outcomes. **Console** remains available
when logs exist. Tab and scrolling continue to work during execution, including
in compact 80 by 24 terminals.

![Live Collection Runner in an 80 by 24 terminal showing a passed request, a running request, a pending request, and Stop](../../assets/runner-live-80x24.png)

## Stop, browse, and return

Press **s** in the Runner or its result detail overlay, click **Stop**, or
activate the focused Stop action with **Enter** or **Space**. The command
palette also offers **Stop Collection Run**. The stopping state remains visible
until cancellation and cookie cleanup finish.

Completed results and finished diagnostics stay available. Cancelled executions
remain distinct from failures and selected requests that never started. Stop
cannot undo HTTP effects already sent.

Press **Escape** to browse elsewhere while the run continues, then **F5** to
return to the current workspace's live, partial, or final results. Read-only
navigation and appearance controls remain available. Execution-affecting edits,
additional sends, collection switches and reloads, and environment, cookie, or
transport changes stay locked until cleanup finishes.

Results stay in the current Runner workspace until a new run or workspace
reset. They are not saved as per-collection history or retained on disk. Read
the [Collection Runner guide](/docs/guides/collection-runner/) for the full
workflow and controls.

## Recover abandoned cookie locks safely

Cookie jar and encryption-key locks can recover automatically after a confirmed
owner process exits. Recovery requires valid owner metadata, preserves existing
cookies and stored keys, and keeps an abandoned-lock backup. Live or uncertain
owners stay locked; a lock's age is never enough to reclaim it. Environment
transaction locks keep their existing manual recovery workflow.

Windows also retries temporary lock-directory creation contention while
preserving persistent filesystem errors. See
[cookie storage guidance](/docs/guides/cookies/#storage-and-unavailable-jars)
before considering a session reset.

CI and release jobs now use Bun 1.4.3 with matching artifact metadata. Runtime
and development dependencies are refreshed, while OpenTUI remains pinned to
0.5.14. The maintained `noodle-dev` and `noodle-use` skills cover the changed
Runner and cookie workflows. Read the
[full changelog](https://github.com/wilfredinni/noodle/blob/v0.10.1/CHANGELOG.md)
for the release details.
